# Release v0.24.2

Patch release following v0.24.1.

## Changes

Move `FractionSimplification`, `LowestCommonDenominator`, and `LowestCommonNumerator`
from `FractionEquivalence` into the existing `FractionStrategies` family through `partOf`.

`FractionEquivalence` describes equal rational values expressed by different fractions.
The three procedures are methods for working with fractions, not constituents that define
that principle. Their existing definitions fit `FractionStrategies`, which already covers
methods involving fraction magnitude, equivalence, and arithmetic relationships.
See [ONT-S1](../structure.md#ont-s1--partof-organizes-constituents).

## Compatibility

No descriptor is added, removed, renamed, or redefined. All dimensions, specialization edges,
constraints, progression relations, and client helper signatures remain unchanged.

Structural navigation changes: the three procedures now traverse through `FractionStrategies`
to `FractionArithmetic`, rather than through `FractionEquivalence`. Inverse child lists change
accordingly. `FractionEquivalence` now has no constituent children. Stored structural ancestry
and relation-derived embeddings may need refreshing; direct annotations and specialization
inheritance do not change.

## Validation and publication

Both client suites check the strategy family through direct parents, inverse access, structural
navigation, and absence of specialization inheritance. The supplied Docker build validates the
sources, generates both clients, runs their tests, and packages the release.
