# Release v0.24.1

Patch release following v0.24.0.

## Changes

- Add `Area.CircularShapes` as a field within `TwoDimensionalObjects`.
- Place `Circle`, `HalfCircle`, and `QuarterCircle` within that field through `partOf`.
- Clarify `Circle` as knowledge of complete circles and the circular regions they enclose.
- Correct documentation examples that treated physical object composition as sufficient
  evidence for composition between concepts.

A semicircular piece can be part of a circular object without making the HalfCircle concept
a constituent or specialization of the Circle concept. The new parent organizes three distinct
concepts without making one a substitute for another. See
[ONT-S1](../structure.md#ont-s1--partof-organizes-constituents).

## Compatibility

No existing descriptor identifier, dimension, specialization edge, constraint, progression edge,
or client helper signature changes. Circle remains a complete-circle claim; a HalfCircle or
QuarterCircle annotation does not imply it. The new parent is structural, not an automatically
added annotation.

Structural traversal changes: HalfCircle and QuarterCircle no longer traverse through Circle,
and all three traverse through CircularShapes to TwoDimensionalObjects. Review stored ancestry
and relation-derived embeddings when adopting this version. Circle definition-dependent
classification context also needs refreshing; direct identifiers do not need replacing.

combined navigation, and absence of inheritance between the three shape concepts.
