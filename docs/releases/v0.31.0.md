# Release v0.31.0

This release consolidates the mathematics extensions developed since v0.30.0, from school
foundations through selected university-entry topics. The authored ontology contains
**1,441 descriptors and 7,668 assertions**, compared with 766 descriptors and 4,123 assertions
in v0.30.0: 677 descriptors are added and two are retired.

## Mathematics coverage

- **Formal mathematics and algebra:** expression roles and notation, arithmetic and algebraic
  expressions, shared dependence characteristics, equivalence and rewriting methods, constraint
  solving, polynomial results, and algebraic representations. ArithmeticExpression and
  AlgebraicExpression distinguish the observable expression form; functions and relations
  remain within Algebra.
- **Analysis and calculus:** function types and properties, sequence and series properties,
  limit guarantees, and separate Calculus families for differentiation, integration, applications,
  approximation, curve calculus, and elementary differential equations. Finite calculations do
  not by themselves establish convergence or justify an infinite limiting step.
- **Number theory and complex numbers:** divisibility, primes and factorization, congruences,
  number-system properties and number-theoretic functions; complex arithmetic, geometric and
  exponential forms, roots and polynomial relationships.
- **Linear algebra:** a distinct root for vector and matrix arithmetic, scalar products,
  cross products, determinants, inverses, linear systems and transformations.
- **Probability, statistics and combinatorics:** probability foundations, random variables and
  distributions, descriptive statistics, inference, counting methods, finite Markov chains,
  and explicit absorption and convergence conditions and guarantees.
- **Geometry, logic and graph theory:** spatial and Euclidean foundations, congruence and
  similarity criteria, circle and triangle results, conics, construction techniques,
  logical connectives and quantification, and a separate GraphTheory root.
- **Scopes:** more precise numeric, expression, exponent and representation contexts;
  algebraic and probability representations, geometric nets, sign and variation tables,
  construction instruments, and digital/software tools. A tool Scope identifies context;
  construction-method knowledge has its own geometry Areas.

The [curriculum audit](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/blob/c2fc224e261830ef4ffdb4b95dc7d13c2942b41c/docs/plan/coverage-audit-2026-10-21.md) records mappings to CCSS and
selected advanced Bavarian and French courses, remaining optional extensions, and source
and prerequisite limits. These mappings do not certify complete curriculum implementation
or dataset coverage. Programming and cryptography are excluded from the agreed extension scope.

## Adoption from v0.30.0

This is a semantic vocabulary update. Consumers should review changed definitions, ancestry
and labeling eligibility as well as generated enum keys.

| Change | Consumer action |
| --- | --- |
| MathematicalLaws becomes ArithmeticLaws under Arithmetic | Update organizer references to ArithmeticLaws. Direct annotations still require the particular evidenced law or equivalence concept, rather than the organizer. |
| ParenthesesUsage retired | Review GroupingSymbol together with OrderOfOperations and the relevant expression concept; retain only claims supported by the content. |
| Algebra, Analysis, Calculus, Logic, SetTheory and Statistics gain constituent children | These become organizational nodes and are no longer eligible direct labels. Replace existing uses with evidenced constituent concepts. |
| DivisibilityRules, Factorization and MathematicalExpression become eligible | Existing narrower concepts can now support these claims where their specialization permits it; eligibility alone does not add a label to content. Factorization still concerns integer product decomposition. |
| Existing descriptors gain revised definitions or relations | Reassess affected annotations and specialization-based matching against the released snapshot. A partOf edge is organizational membership, not capability inheritance. |

Direct Statistics annotations in particular need an evidence-based choice among statistical
concepts, rather than a blanket identifier substitution. The
[statistics adoption record](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/blob/c2fc224e261830ef4ffdb4b95dc7d13c2942b41c/docs/plan/probability-statistics-combinatorics-draft.md#adoption-effects)
and [number-theory adoption record](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/blob/c2fc224e261830ef4ffdb4b95dc7d13c2942b41c/docs/plan/number-theory-basics-draft.md#implementation-and-adoption--2026-10-10)
describe the affected boundaries.

Consumers of intermediate previews must also account for changes to identifiers introduced
during this release cycle:

- NumericalExpression becomes ArithmeticExpression, with an arithmetic-only boundary;
  GroupedExpression and VariableExpression are replaced by composition with structural roles.
- BinomialIdentities is split into BinomialSquareIdentities and DifferenceOfSquaresIdentity.
  Select one or both according to evidence; the two have different specialization paths.
- GraphicalSolutionRepresentation becomes the broader GraphicalAlgebraicRepresentation.
  Solution-set or solving knowledge remains a separate claim.
- ArithmeticFunctions becomes NumberTheoreticFunctions, specifically for number-theoretic
  integer functions rather than general numerical functions.

These retired identifiers are not compatibility aliases. The
[algebra design record](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/blob/c2fc224e261830ef4ffdb4b95dc7d13c2942b41c/docs/plan/algebra-family-draft.md#2026-10-10-migration-and-adoption)
documents the preview refinements in detail.

## Libraries and release assets

The TypeScript and Python public query APIs remain unchanged. Generated descriptor enums,
definitions and relation data reflect the new ontology. TypeScript snapshot generation now
checks and widens individual records before assembling the array, avoiding the compiler's
union-complexity limit for the larger ontology without changing the public snapshot type.

The release provides the four authored Turtle sources, compiled RDF/XML, edugraph-ts.tgz,
and the Python wheel and source distribution. The tag workflow runs the full Docker build,
ontology and documentation checks, generated-client tests, package verification, and Python
compatibility tests across supported versions and platforms.
