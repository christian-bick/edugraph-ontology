# Dataset adoption handover for ontology v0.32.0

This is the implementation handover for upgrading the dataset from stable **v0.31.0** to the
v0.32.0 candidate. Dataset migration has not been performed. Publishing the ontology does not
establish that the dataset, its cached judgments, or trained models have adopted it.

Use the [identifier mapping](#identifier-migration), [eligibility changes](#labeling-eligibility),
and [definition review](#definition-and-example-review) for implementation. Then check
[relations and matching](#relations-matching-and-new-vocabulary) and complete the
[validation handover](#artifacts-model-inputs-and-validation-handover). The
[retained decisions](#retained-decisions-and-preview-only-compatibility) prevent unintended
adoption of proposals that were withdrawn or parked.

## Baseline and scope

The inspected dataset checkout is `edugraph-content`, with Git identity
[`2e08f3654d30cc1567f701c4bc78b1b68f60d568`](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-dataset/tree/2e08f3654d30cc1567f701c4bc78b1b68f60d568).
Its `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, and installed `edugraph-ts/package.json` all identify
**0.31.0**, downloaded from the stable v0.31.0 release. The locked package integrity is
`sha512-HkkdpsRSri+i685qog5mdT6uOc65GjXLHbnhbJH7/Gu/epQVmYdBwHTXkHf6Qk4GGXGlYuPkTIxBsEAtxwJ6EA==`.
The 33 source files containing removed names were checked against that Git revision and match.

The ontology comparison covers the complete four authored Turtle sources from `v0.31.0` to
merged main [`337e1ec8b3fc06094a8ace4deb356ca2d793fa01`](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/commit/337e1ec8b3fc06094a8ace4deb356ca2d793fa01),
not only the final consolidation branch. Source-aware snapshots contain **1,441 → 1,489 descriptors**
and **7,668 → 7,934 assertions**. There are **23 renames**, **one retirement**, and **49 genuinely
new descriptors**. Identifier and definition changes are in Areas; the Scope `GeometricNet` has an example-only change. Ability sources and the schema are unchanged.

The definition inventory below is exhaustive for retained concepts after matching the renames:
20 existing-IRI definitions and three renamed definitions change; `GeometricNet` additionally
has a comment-only change. Relation changes also affect inherited claims and matching even where
definitions are unchanged. Label eligibility was independently compared through the installed
and candidate libraries' `bundledContext.inspectLabel`.

Read the ontology's [change review](../change-review.md),
[versioned annotation guidance](../annotations-and-models.md#interpreting-annotations-across-ontology-versions),
and [ONT-E7](../content-evidence.md#ont-e7--label-observable-descriptors-not-organizational-nodes).
The dataset owns its implementation and validation workflow; its
[`docs/label-architecture.md`](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-dataset/blob/2e08f3654d30cc1567f701c4bc78b1b68f60d568/docs/label-architecture.md) and [`docs/target-spec.md`](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-dataset/blob/2e08f3654d30cc1567f701c4bc78b1b68f60d568/docs/target-spec.md) define the relevant contracts.

## Retained decisions and preview-only compatibility

`Iteration` and `IteratedOperation` remain separate and unchanged. **Do not replace or drop
`IteratedOperation` during this release adoption.** The inspected dataset uses it in three
generator specs (`arithmetic-ops-pairs`, `number-array`, `fraction-arithmetic`) and seven authored
target permutations across Grade 2 and Grade 4. Its equal-addends constraint is operational:
[`src/generators/arithmetic/arithmetic-ops-pairs/spec.ts`](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-dataset/blob/2e08f3654d30cc1567f701c4bc78b1b68f60d568/src/generators/arithmetic/arithmetic-ops-pairs/spec.ts#L39) resolves it into
`requireEqualAddends`. Replacing it with `Iteration` or deleting it would lose that constraint
unless the consumer deliberately represents it elsewhere.

The earlier concern remains conditional: two equal addends such as `7 + 7` use one binary addition
and do not automatically demonstrate repeated application. Repeated arrays and whole-number
multiples of fractions are different cases. Reopen only if reviewing current rendered evidence
establishes a concrete defect; no consolidation or dataset relabeling is mandated by v0.32.0.
Keep any future change scoped to its targets, capabilities, equality constraints and visible
evidence, then regenerate its dependent artifacts. See [`src/spec/ccss/grade-02.ts`](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-dataset/blob/2e08f3654d30cc1567f701c4bc78b1b68f60d568/src/spec/ccss/grade-02.ts#L89)
and [`src/spec/ccss/grade-04.ts`](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-dataset/blob/2e08f3654d30cc1567f701c4bc78b1b68f60d568/src/spec/ccss/grade-04.ts#L578).

Other deliberately retained decisions include `Modulo` as a general remainder operation without
one fixed numeric domain or new `ModularArithmetic` parent; unchanged `Factorial` placement;
unchanged `IntegerStrategies`, fraction-strategy coverage, `CircularPermutationCounting`,
`RootIndex`, `ShapeReflection`, `NumericComparison`, `VectorAlgebra`, `LogicalEquivalence`, and
`RandomVariable` / `RandomVariableTheory`. Do not implement the parked PowerFunction/dependence,
operation-integration, unordered-selection specialization, or iteration proposals without an
actual defect and a separately agreed change.

For consumers that adopted intermediate previews, inspect their exact snapshot separately:
`PercentageApplication` was an intermediate name for `PercentageRateApplication`, and
`FiniteMarkovChainTheory` was introduced then removed. The latter has no replacement label;
its general Markov constituents remain directly in `StochasticProcesses`, while three absorption
topics are grouped under `MarkovChainAbsorption`. Neither intermediate name exists in v0.31.0,
so neither is a stable-v0.31 migration. The previously released v0.31 adoption work and its
older-preview names belong to that earlier release, not this delta.
