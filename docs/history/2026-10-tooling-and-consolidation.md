# Tooling and consolidation through October 2026

The authoring references established stable descriptor, evidence, structure, relation, and
change-review rules. TypeScript checks now cover schema contracts, authored relation choices,
structural ordering and cycles, child roles, progression cycles, and documentation references.
Label eligibility remains a consumer-facing query; semantic correctness and content evidence
remain review work.

Shared TypeScript and typed Python clients gained immutable supplied-snapshot queries, faithful
RDF parsing, explicit unknown handling, packaged references, and generated compatibility adapters.
TypeScript supplies ontology assessments. Docker verifies both clients and installed package
boundaries. Definition/comment normalization and combined statement rendering were delivered
through the versioned releases. Current operational details live in [DOCS.md](../../DOCS.md),
and remaining work in the [library roadmap](../plan/library-roadmap.md).

The hierarchy cleanup recorded in
[PR #86](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/86) and
[PR #87](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/87) removed
ComputationalMathematics, consolidated mathematical notation and set families, connected numeric
and system substitution, expanded percentage topics, grouped decimal methods, and aligned lines,
rays and arcs. Redundant Concept suffixes were removed; GeometricFoundations replaced the broader
organizer name, and NormalVector gained Vector specialization.

The active plans retain unresolved modeling, adoption, and performance decisions. Detailed
implementation, migration, and verification records remain in Git history and
[release notes](../releases/), rather than in the active backlog.
