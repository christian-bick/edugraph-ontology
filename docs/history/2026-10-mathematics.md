# Mathematics extensions — October 2026

The October work expanded authored mathematics concepts and their reusable notation,
representation, and tool contexts. The detailed proposals, examples, validation records, and
curriculum mappings remain in Git history.

- Algebra and Analysis were reworked around compositional expression, function, constraint,
  dependence, and property concepts in [the initial implementation](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/commit/10a4036)
  and [PR #68](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/68).
  [Number-theory foundations](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/commit/1b9ecc3)
  and [Pythagoras/unit-dimensionality additions](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/commit/702714b)
  followed.
- Complex numbers, further functions/trigonometry, linear algebra, calculus, and
  probability/statistics/combinatorics were extended through
  [PR #71](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/71),
  [#72](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/72),
  [#73](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/73),
  [#74](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/74), and
  [#75](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/75).
- [Spatial geometry](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/76),
  [further complex results](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/77),
  [graph foundations](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/78),
  [logic](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/79),
  [conics](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/80),
  [number-theoretic results](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/81), and
  [Euclidean construction/theorem families](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/82)
  were added.
  Subsequent batches supplied cross products, finite arithmetic sums and sequence-limit
  guarantees ([#83](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/83)),
  interval tables, probability histograms and tool Scopes
  ([#84](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/84)), and finite Markov-chain
  absorption/convergence ([#85](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/85)).

Later [hierarchy consolidation](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/pull/86)
and [geometric-foundations refinement](https://github.com/christian-bick/edugraph-ontology/commit/c2fc224)
adjusted organization and identifiers. Current definitions and structure are in the authored
Turtle; completed implementation does not establish consumer adoption or curriculum coverage.
Unresolved decisions and optional continuations are retained in
[mathematics extensions](../plan/mathematics-extensions.md).
