## Scope decisions retained

Programming, encryption/decryption, key construction, and cryptographic protocols, including
RSA, remain excluded from the selected mathematics extension. Mathematical algorithms and
their correctness remain in scope independently of code. Digital-tool contexts do not by
themselves establish programming knowledge or general application proficiency.

Mathematical coding theory needs a separate scope decision; it is not automatically excluded
as programming or cryptography. Arbitrary-set functions and deeper optional mathematical
topics remain choices in the [extension backlog](mathematics-extensions.md).
