# France advanced lycée mathematics coverage audit

[Audit conclusions and method](coverage-audit-2026-10-21.md).

Ontology baseline: `0af4aba97731e49cbff3ac51ca9ef519d174a945`. Audit date: 2026-10-21.

At the audit baseline, mathematical coverage was substantial but incomplete. Core mathematical gaps included arithmetic finite-sum identities and the monotone bounded-convergence and increasing-unbounded-divergence guarantees. Definition/representation follow-ups included signed-infinite limit rules and sign/variation tables. Subsequent implementation progress is recorded in the [main audit roadmap](coverage-audit-2026-10-21.md#suggested-implementation-order-and-exit-criteria).

Scope decision, 2026-10-22: [programming is intentionally excluded](coverage-audit-2026-10-21.md#programming-excluded-from-the-current-extension-scope) from the current ontology extension. Programming rows and the historical counts below remain evidence of curricular requirements, not pending work within the agreed scope. In mixed rows, the mathematical component remains relevant; its code implementation is excluded. Broad procedural Abilities and software-tool Scopes do not fill programming knowledge gaps.

The same scope decision excludes encryption/decryption, key concepts and cryptographic protocols
in FR-EXP-N09, FR-EXP-N22 and FR-EXP-N25. Their historical judgments remain below, but these
are not pending work. Markov-chain absorption and convergence remain in scope; FR-EXP-M14
is included in the next mathematics batch despite its optional curricular status.

## Method and limits

- Current 2026-27 audit combines the effective première2026, terminale2019 and expertes2019 programmes. This does not claim the current terminale cohort studied première2026.
- Terminale2026 is effective2027-28; only a separate forward comparison is included and excluded from all current counts.
- Première explicitly inherits all seconde automatismes. The full seconde2026 programme and lower-school prerequisites are not row-audited here.
- Mathematics complémentaires, technological trajectories, national examination task specifications and optional history suggestions are outside this chosen advanced trajectory.
- Demonstration items are separately recorded. Programmes prescribe exemplary demonstrations encountered in varied modes; required status here means listed programme expectation, not that each proof must be independently reproduced unaided.
- Algorithm examples are illustrative and optional extensions are separate. Mathematical algorithms and Python/list/program semantics are not conflated.
- Rows normalize nearby content/capability clauses only where they require the same mathematical knowledge and outcome. They are evidence units, not source-bullet counts; totals are not a percentage of syllabus completeness.
- No ontology root or organizer was used as evidence. ComputationalMathematics is eligible but broad and is listed only as insufficient context for precise programming gaps.
- Mapped proof/communication abilities identify the cognitive outcome; they do not establish theorem-specific knowledge or actual learner performance.
- External URLs were verified through official ministry/Eduscol sources; PDF page numbers are physical one-based pages.

Judgments:

- **direct**: One or more existing precise descriptors explicitly state the required knowledge; ordinary use does not need a separate topic.
- **composition**: The required competence can be identified by a combination of eligible knowledge, scope and outcome descriptors. This is semantic representability, not an implemented exercise or pedagogical guarantee.
- **gap**: A distinct required concept/method or program-language knowledge is not stated by eligible descriptors. Related descriptors listed are context, not proof of coverage.
- **definition-or-representation gap**: An existing close descriptor or combination exists, but its exact definition does not explicitly reach this rule, convention or representation. Often a concise refinement rather than a new family.
- **unverifiable**: Programme wording or the audited source boundary does not determine an exact claim of coverage.
